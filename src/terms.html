4. Data and analytics notice

What we collect when you have an account:

  • Account details: your name, email address, organisation, country and how you plan to use OBQ.
  • Sign-in records: the time of each sign-in, the sign-in method, your interface language and your browser or device type.
  • Quiz set-up records: each time you start a quiz we store information about the set-up: the event name, your name and organisation, the date, the number of participants, the quiz language, the books or scope chosen, the quiz mode and style, the question source, the interface language, the number of questions and the start time. This helps us run the service and count how OBQ is used. It does not include the names or points of participants.
  • Question banks you share: if you choose Share on a question bank, its questions, its name and language, your name and your organisation are stored on our server and shown to other signed-in Quiz Masters, who can add the bank to their own question banks. Share only questions you wrote or translated or may share. The text of a Bible version is not shared unless you tick that you may. You can stop sharing at any time, and an administrator may hide or remove a bank that does not follow these terms.
  • Messages you send us: to use the contact form you confirm your registered email address with a one-time confirmation (a link, or a code) that we send to it. We then receive what you type (your name, email address and message), the topic you choose, your organisation and country from your profile, the app version, your interface language and your browser type. We use this only to reply to you and to improve the app.
  • Analytics you choose to share: in the Analytics Dashboard you can press Share my analytics with the developers. This sends one snapshot of the activity numbers shown there (for example quizzes run, participants entered in quiz set-ups, languages used, questions asked, and the number of questions and Bible texts you have stored) together with your name, organisation and country. It never includes participant names, points or the questions themselves. Nothing is sent unless you press the button, and you can withdraw your snapshot at any time from the same page.
  • Saved items: if you are signed in online, your saved quizzes and question banks may be stored with your account so you can use them on other devices.

Verse of the Day: when the site is set up for it, the verse is fetched from Bible Brain (biblebrain.com) in your interface language. The request contains the verse reference and the site’s API key, and your device’s address as for any web request; it contains nothing about you or your quizzes.

Usage analytics (optional, with your separate consent): only if you say yes (when you sign up, in the choice we show you, or in Settings), we collect anonymous usage analytics with Google Analytics for Firebase: for example which screens are used, how many quizzes are started and finished, and which languages and scopes are chosen. We use this only to improve OBQ. Without your consent no analytics are collected. You can change your choice at any time in Settings.

Translating questions: if you use the feature that puts the OBQ questions into the language of a Bible text, the English question texts are sent to the translation service you choose, with your own key for that service, from your browser. We do not receive them or your key. That service has its own terms and privacy rules. The Bible text you add stays on your device.

What stays on your device: the names of participants and their points, your results history, your Bible texts, the photo of the winners (it is kept only in memory and forgotten when the next quiz ends), your settings and, if you use it, your own Firebase connection details are kept on your device. They are not sent to us. They are sent to a Firebase project only if you connect your own project and choose to send results there.

Device storage: the app stores information in your browser (for example your settings, saved quizzes and sign-in session) so that it works and remembers your choices. This storage is needed to provide the app you asked for. Analytics storage is used only if you agree.

5. AI-generated content, translations and accuracy

Some of the content in OBQ is created or translated with the help of artificial intelligence (AI) tools and machine translation. This includes the demonstration questions and other content in languages other than English, the translations of the app's screens and messages, and any question bank that you make with the Generate questions feature or that another Quiz Master shares with you.

  • It can be wrong. AI and machine translation can make mistakes: unnatural or misleading wording, wrong answers, wrong Bible references, or answers that look right but are not. Automatically generated or translated content has not been checked unless a person has reviewed it.
  • You must check it before using it with the public. Before you use generated or translated questions with a public audience, you (the Quiz Master) must read them, compare them with the Bible text and correct them. You are responsible for what you present.
  • Theological accuracy has not been checked. We have not checked the theological accuracy of each question in the built-in question banks, in any language. A Bible scholar, or a qualified person in your church or organisation, should review a question bank before it is used widely.
  • Shared banks. A question bank shared by another Quiz Master is provided by its owner. We do not review it. Treat it in the same way as generated content: check it first.
  • If you find a mistake in a question or a translation, please tell us with the contact form so that it can be corrected.
